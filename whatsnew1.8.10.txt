[features]
Torches. Place one and it lights the area around it, brightness 14.
Light now spreads from one chunk into the next instead of stopping dead at the edge.
Sky light and torch light are added together now, the way Minecraft does it.
Fake shading, a cheap lighting effect you can turn on in options. Off by default.
Water shimmers when that option is on -- slow pale highlights drifting across the surface.
