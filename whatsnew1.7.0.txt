# v1.7.0 ONLY. The lead item is the one that matters: v1.6.0 could not start.
# Player-facing prose only - it wraps at 61 characters on a 400px screen.
# The developer-facing story lives in CHANGELOG.md.

[features]
New terrain. It is carved out of the world rather than drawn as a heightmap.
That means real caves, overhangs and cliff faces instead of hills with holes.
Water. There is a sea now, and lakes. It is still and does not flow yet.
Tall grass grows on grass blocks. It is the first plant that is not a cube.
The surface is redone. Grass, dirt and sand now depend on depth and slope.
Worlds you already have keep the terrain they were made with. Nothing is reshaped.

[fixes]
Version 1.6.0 could not start at all. It showed a black screen and nothing else.
If you installed 1.6.0, this is the update that makes the game run again.
Parts of the world could fail to appear and never come back, leaving holes in it.
There is now room for every piece of the world you can see at the furthest setting.
Trees no longer try to grow underwater.
