[features]
Chests: craft an 8-slot storage block and place it to stash items.
Chests work with friends on a server too, not just solo.
Multiplayer chest transfers need a server running v1.9.10 or newer.
A v1.9.9 server lets you join and place or break chests, but not move items in or out.
Servers on v1.6.0 through v1.9.8 refuse the join outright - update your server first.
Older servers (pre-v1.6.0) still join fine, without chest sync, exactly as before.
World list: press delete twice to confirm, so a stray tap cannot wipe a world by accident.
World list: rename any world right from the list, no need to load it first.
L and R now cycle your hotbar slot instead of stepping the render distance live.
Render distance moved to the pause menu, where it always steps by choice, not by accident.
A small readout under the crosshair now names whatever block you are looking at.
Inventory: split a stack in two, merge two stacks, or quick-move a slot with one press.
Nothing in this build has run on real 3DS hardware yet.
