[features]
Every new world now gets its own seed, so no two worlds look the same any more.
Six kinds of place to find: tundra, taiga, plains, forest, desert and jungle.
Snow, ice, cactus, dead bushes and ferns, each growing where they belong.
Trees are different in every biome now - round crowns, tall conifers, broad jungle ones.
Joining a server that builds its world differently now tells you, instead of going wrong.

[fixes]
You can climb out of the water again. Swim to a shore, press into it and you are out.
Gentle bobbing is back on the water surface, much calmer than before.
The battery gauge blinks when the battery is nearly flat.
Lighting after a big water spill no longer stutters the game while it catches up.
Old worlds open exactly as they were. Nothing in this update reshapes them.
