[features]
Your bag, the crafting grid, chests and the furnace are one bar now, not four separate screens.
Only the tabs that apply are there — the chest tab shows up while a chest is open.
L and R change tab, the D-pad or circle pad moves the cursor, A takes and B puts down.
The pause menu and title screen use the same bar, so everything moves the same way.
While the bar is open you stop walking, so you cannot wander off while sorting your bag.

[fixes]
A stack you were holding no longer follows you out of a chest that closed under you.
Left and Right now work on every row of the Options screen, not just the sliders.
