[features]
Six ores to mine: coal, iron, gold, redstone, lapis and diamond, in veins underground.
Ore appears in new worlds only. Existing saves keep their terrain and everything you built.
A frame timing readout, under Pause, Options, Debug, showing where each frame's time goes.

[fixes]
Torches can now be crafted: four from one coal ore. Until now there was no way to get one.
The world generates faster, and working out what is hidden behind a hill is much cheaper.
Crash reports are kept instead of being deleted the next time you start the game.
