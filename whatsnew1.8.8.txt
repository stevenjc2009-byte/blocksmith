[features]
Every biome now has its own colour, so you can see where one ends and the next begins.
Birch and spruce trees, with their own logs, planks and leaves. Each breaks at its own speed.
Four flowers, taller grass and dead bushes worth looking at, scattered to suit each biome.
Apples grow in oak and birch trees, and sometimes fall when you break the leaves.
Sound is on at last: blocks break, blocks place, footsteps. The sun and moon move too.
A Version history button on the update screen shows what every past update changed.

[fixes]
Cactus could not be broken at all, and five other blocks could not be picked up. All fixed.
Standing grass was a bright lime green next to the darker grass block. Repainted to match.
The debug "see" distance was reading the wrong number and ignored your render distance.
