[features]
Health and hunger, shown as two rows of pips along the bottom of the touch screen.
Fall damage. Drops over three blocks hurt, and landing in water cancels it entirely.
Eat an apple by holding it and pressing the place button. It restores four hunger.
Starving hurts but cannot kill you - it stops at one health. Falling can kill you.
Dying puts you back at spawn with full health and hunger. Your inventory is kept.
A rebindable Eat button, default ZR. ZR is New 3DS only; the place button works on both.
Health and hunger are saved with your world, and on a server the server owns them.
