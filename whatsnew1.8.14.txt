[features]
Animals. Pigs, cows, chickens and sheep now live in the world and wander about on their own.
Which animals turn up depends on the biome. Plains and forest have all four, desert has none.
Hit an animal and it runs away from you rather than standing there and taking it.
Killing one drops raw meat straight into your bag - porkchop, beef, chicken or mutton.
Eat raw meat like an apple. Pork and beef restore three hunger, chicken and mutton two.
Raw meat is a block as well as a food, so you can place it and break it like anything else.
Aiming at an animal now comes first, so punching one no longer breaks the wall behind it.
New 3DS keeps up to 32 animals loaded at once. Old 3DS keeps 16, so both stay smooth.
Update your server too. Against an older one the new blocks silently turn into air.
