[fixes]
Reloading a world dropped you inside your own blocks, so the ground looked like it had gone.
You now come back exactly where you were standing, facing the same way.
Tall grass can be broken now. Before, swinging at it did nothing at all.
Frame rate drops while walking are much smaller. The worst stutters are down by half.
Worlds with saved changes open faster. The save file is no longer reread for every column.
Water no longer holds you under. You sink slowly, swim up, and move slower in it.

[features]
Nothing new to look at in this one. It is a repair release.
