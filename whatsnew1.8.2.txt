# v1.8.2 ONLY. This release is the water rework - how it spreads, how tall it sits,
# seeing through it, and swimming to the surface. Nothing else is claimed here.
# The bigger texture atlas is deliberately NOT here: no new block uses it yet, so
# there is nothing a player can see or do differently for it.
# Player-facing prose only - it wraps at 61 characters on a 400px screen.
# Items are capped at 95 characters each by source/app/whatsnew.h.
# The developer-facing story lives in CHANGELOG.md.

[features]
Water spreads a bit at a time now. Pour it out and you can watch it find its way.
It ends up in the same place as before, it just takes longer to get there.
Water sits a little below the top of its block, so there is a lip where it meets land.
You can see through water. Look down into a lake and the bottom is there.
Swimming up to the surface settles now instead of bobbing. Hold the button to float.

[fixes]
Standing right on the line between two areas could blink a hole in the world.
That was easiest to catch underground, where a cave wall would vanish for a moment.
The icon on the Home Menu plays its sound now instead of sitting there silent.
