[features]
Chunks load nearest-first, so the ground under you fills in before terrain behind you.
Caves and terrain generate over twice as fast, so the world keeps up better as you move.
Groundwork for a second world-building thread on the New 3DS. Nothing changes yet.

[fixes]
Lighting could come out wrong after a block was broken or placed. Both consoles, since 1.8.0.
A block you broke or placed could silently never reach other players, splitting the world.
Joining a server on a different version let you in to a world full of holes. Now it refuses.
You could break and place blocks from inside the pause menu, and water kept flowing.
