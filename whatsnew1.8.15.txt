[features]
Furnace. Craft one from 8 stone in the crafting list, same as anything else you make.
Aim at a placed furnace and press Place to open it - the same button you build with.
Put fuel in one slot and raw meat in the other. Planks burn 15 seconds, a log burns 60.
Cooking an item takes 10 seconds. A lit furnace with nothing to cook just burns the fuel.
Cook porkchop, beef, chicken or mutton. Cooked meat restores far more hunger than raw does.
Cooked porkchop and beef restore 8 hunger, cooked chicken and mutton 6 - beats an apple's 4.
Eat cooked meat the same way as anything else - hold it and press Place.
Update your server too. Against an older one the game now refuses to connect at all.
