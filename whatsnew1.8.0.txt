[features]
Water flows. Dig into a lake and it pours into the hole instead of sitting there.
Shallow water is drawn shallow. Eight depths, so you can see where a pool gets deeper.
Water falls a block wide and pools where it lands. A waterfall does not thin out on the way.
Take away the block a flow came from and the whole flow drains away.

[fixes]
Water that only changed depth now redraws. Before, the shape on screen could go stale.
