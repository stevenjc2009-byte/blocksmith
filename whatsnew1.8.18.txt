[fixes]
A glyph could render garbage on real hardware. The font texture is now flushed after loading.
Fixed a real race where two threads relighting the same area at once could corrupt its light.
Fixed a stack overflow risk on the worker thread from an oversized buffer. No known crash yet.
Chicken wings and tails are easier to make out now, and cows are shaded on their underside.
The updater's error screen now shows free memory too, in case it is running out mid-check.

[features]
Zombies and skeletons. They spawn in the dark and in caves, and they can hurt and kill you.
Torches keep them away and make them wander off if one gets close.
Leaves take their biome's colour now, the same as grass and dirt.
A biome border now fades between the two colours instead of stepping sharply between them.
The freeze is not confirmed fixed. If it happens again, stage.txt on the card says more now.
