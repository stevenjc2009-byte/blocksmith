# v1.8.1 ONLY. This release is the timed block break and its crack animation.
# Player-facing prose only - it wraps at 61 characters on a 400px screen.
# Items are capped at 95 characters each by source/app/whatsnew.h.
# The developer-facing story lives in CHANGELOG.md.

[features]
Breaking a block takes time now. Hold the break button instead of tapping it.
Cracks spread across the block while you mine it, so you can see how far along you are.
Every block has its own hardness. Tall grass comes out at once, stone takes a while.
Let go or look away and the block heals. Progress does not follow your crosshair around.

[fixes]
A block you cannot carry is refused as soon as you start, not after the whole animation.
